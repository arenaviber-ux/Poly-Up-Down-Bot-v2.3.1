♻️ Capital Recycling —

Why both are on by default. Polymarket's CLOB has no redemption endpoint, and GET /balance-allowance reports collateral only — winning shares are ERC-1155 tokens that do not count toward it. A bot that only ever buys converts its whole float into unredeemable tokens and then stops trading while realised P&L keeps rising. The exit recycles capital in the same tick (selling at ~99c costs ~1c, and the taker fee at that price is ~0.07c/share); the sweeper collects whatever the exit could not act on. Turning both off in live mode will strand your balance.
